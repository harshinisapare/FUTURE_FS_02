# 🚀 Forge CRM — Client Lead Management System

A modern full-stack **Customer Relationship Management (CRM)** application designed for agencies, freelancers, and startups to efficiently manage client leads, monitor sales pipelines, and convert prospects into customers. The application provides a centralized dashboard for tracking lead progress, managing follow-ups, and visualizing business performance through real-time analytics.

## ✨ Features

- 🔐 **Secure Authentication** – Role-based admin login with protected dashboard access.
- 📋 **Lead Management** – Perform full CRUD operations on client leads.
- 📈 **Lead Pipeline** – Track leads through different stages: **New**, **Contacted**, and **Converted**.
- 📝 **Notes & Follow-ups** – Maintain timestamped notes and interaction history for each lead.
- 📊 **KPI Dashboard** – View real-time metrics including total leads, conversion rate, and pipeline value.
- 📉 **Analytics** – Interactive charts for lead sources, status distribution, and weekly lead activity.
- 🔍 **Search & Filtering** – Quickly search leads by name, email, or company and filter by status or source.
- 🌙 **Dark/Light Mode** – Switch seamlessly between themes.
- 📱 **Responsive Design** – Optimized for desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.
- ☁️ **Cloud Database** – Lead information is securely stored using MongoDB Atlas.
